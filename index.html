How is the score calculated?

The overall score blends two signals:

  • Skill coverage (65%) — the share of curated skills found in the job description that also appear in your CV, using a taxonomy of common Data Analyst / Data Engineer / Data Scientist tools, languages, and concepts.
  • Text similarity (35%) — TF-IDF cosine similarity between the full text of your CV and the job description, capturing broader overlap beyond the fixed skill list.
